Collection 001 / China / A personal diary

The Shanghai
Diary

From my first inspiration to the last little detail. Here is how I started turning a skyscraper into something I could wear.

The first inspiration
Chapter 01 / The city

It starts with a skyline.

Every collection needs a beginning, and mine starts with Shanghai. I love the feeling of looking up at a skyline and finding one building that seems to have its own personality.

Shanghai Tower caught my attention with its twisting shape. I kept coming back to the idea of height, movement and light. Before thinking about a dress, I wanted to understand what made this tower feel so different.

For me, these first images are about finding a mood. I'm not choosing fabrics yet. I'm choosing the story I want the clothes to tell.

Colour direction
Chapter 02 / Palette & texture

Colour before couture.

The colours are where the mood starts to feel real. I keep coming back to silver, cool grey, icy blue and those little reflections you see across glass buildings as the light changes.

I don't want a dress that looks like a literal copy of a tower. I want the colour to remind you of its atmosphere: modern, luminous and a little mysterious. Soft tones will keep the silhouette elegant, while reflective details will bring in the architecture.

Fabric exploration
Chapter 03 / Materials

What would glass feel like?

I'm drawn to fabrics that play with light. Fine transparent layers, delicate metallic textures and materials with a soft shimmer are a way to give the architectural inspiration a fashion identity.

I love the contrast between something structured and something that moves with the body. This part is about finding a fabric language before settling on the final shape of the dress.
