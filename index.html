The philosophy

Every city has
a silhouette.

DressTheWorld is a fashion storytelling project translating internationally recognized architecture into wearable couture, guided by structure, texture, proportion, and the identity of each place.

The creative journey

From building
to ballroom.

A complete design story, filmed from the first inspiration to the final look.

01 / SELECT

The landmark

Choose a recognizable building and study its lines, form and architectural identity.

02 / OBSERVE

The visit

Explore the building's setting and details, capturing the geometry and mood of the place.

03 / DRAW

The sketches

Translate façade patterns, oblique lines and structural elements into fashion designs.

04 / SELECT

The materials

Match textiles, transparent layers, reflective surfaces and structural accents to the concept.

05 / CREATE

The ensemble

Compose an evening dress with complementary gloves and an architectural hat or headpiece.

06 / REVEAL

The fitting

Refine the proportions on the model and reveal the final cocktail-ready couture look.
